## personal top 10 favourite ⚽️ players of all time

### 10. lucas moura 🇧🇷
#### "champions league fairy tale"
<img src="../../../notes/futbol/lucas.png" alt="Lucas Moura" width="1500"/>

#### that hatrick in the champions league semi final against ajax 😮‍💨 i remember being in sixth grade, staring at the live scoreboard when it happened. chills.
<br/>

### 9. khvicha kvaratskhelia 🇬🇪
#### "modern era magician"
<img src="../../../notes/futbol/kvara.png" alt="Khvicha Kvaratskhelia" width="1500"/>

<br/>

### 8. christian eriksen 🇩🇰
#### "great dane"
<img src="../../../notes/futbol/eriksen.png" alt="Christian Eriksen" width="1500"/>

#### "la joya"
